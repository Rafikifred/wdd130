Our History

Our white water rafting company was founded by river enthusiasts who believed that everyone should experience the exhilaration of navigating rapids. What began as a small group of friends running the mighty Padas River grew into a full‑service adventure outfitter.

Today our experienced guides and safety‑first approach ensure an unforgettable trip for families, friends and thrill seekers alike. Join us and become part of our story!
